Research Vision

My research bridges advanced polymer science and environmental stewardship — designing high-performance, circular material systems at the interfaces of natural rubbers, biocomposites, biopolymers, and polymer nanocomposites. I focus on translating materials innovations from lab-scale synthesis to scalable processing for real-world industrial and biomedical applications.

A central theme in my work is waste valorisation: converting agricultural byproducts, biomass, and industrial co-products into functional fillers and reinforcements that reduce dependence on petroleum-derived carbon black. My PhD at IIT Kharagpur established deep expertise in TPU-PDMS nanocomposites and tissue engineering scaffolds; my postdoctoral work at the University of Guelph, Canada (BDDC) extended this into green biocomposites for automotive and packaging applications, in collaboration with Ford R&I Center and National Research Council Canada.

Going forward, I aim to build a research programme around biopolymer-based sustainable materials, bridging molecular-level design and processing science to create the next generation of circular, high-performance composites for industry and medicine.

Future Research Plans

At the Polymer Engineering and Sustainable Composite Lab, I plan to establish an independent research programme that bridges fundamental polymer science with scalable, circular material solutions. The lab will pursue four interconnected thrust areas — each designed to attract national funding and produce impactful, industry-relevant outcomes:

01
Next-generation Biobased Fillers & Composites
Valorising underutilised agricultural and food-processing waste streams (chicken feathers, DDGS, agave, rice husk) into functional biocarbon fillers via controlled pyrolysis. Target applications: automotive, packaging, and infrastructure composites. Collaboration opportunities with Indian rubber and plastics industry.
02
Biopolymer-based Smart & Sustainable Systems
Engineering PHB, PLA, and natural rubber latex-based blends and composites with tailored degradation, mechanical, and barrier properties for single-use plastic replacement and medical device applications. Focus on reactive extrusion and melt-processing scale-up.
03
Polymer Nanocomposites for Biomedical Applications
Extending TPU-PDMS nanocomposite expertise to develop next-generation electrospun scaffolds for bone, cartilage, and wound healing. Incorporating multifunctional nanofillers (halloysite, HA, ZnO) for antibacterial and osteogenic performance. In collaboration with medical institutions.
04
Colloidal Systems & Functional Soft Matter
Developing colloid-based functional coatings, adhesives, and encapsulants inspired by the provisional patent work on water-activated heating systems. Bridging colloid science with polymer engineering for smart packaging and construction applications.
